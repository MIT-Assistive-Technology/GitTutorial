# Corrie

In a few words, answer each question. Don’t think too hard :)

**1. What's your working style?**  
I like brainstorming ideas with people and talking about how they might work, and figuring out how we can combine multiple people's ideas into one that works even better. I also like working on developing ideas on my own to share with the group.

**2. How do you prefer to receive feedback? (i.e. verbal vs. written, how often, anonymously?)**  
I prefer to receive feedback by talking with a person because I think written communication can be misinterpreted more easily.

**3. How do you prefer to give feedback?**  
I prefer to give feedback by talking with a person because I think written communication can be misinterpreted more easily.

**4. How can someone motivate you to do something?**  
I am motivated to do something by knowing why it is important for it to be done and when it should be done by.

**5. What is your preferred method of communication?**  
In-person when possible, or the Assistive Technology Club Slack if not.

**6. Foods you like? :D**  
Gluten-free treats (favorites include Welch's fruit snacks, Popcorners, Breyer's or Talenti ice cream, most things from Verveine)
