**1. What's your working style?**
I like collaborating with other people! But I prefer some time to think about a problem myself and get a full understanding of it before meeting with a larger team and working with everyone, just to be more prepared.

**2. How do you prefer to receive feedback? (i.e. verbal vs. written, how often, anonymously?)**
Verbal, and pretty often, maybe once a week or every other week.

**3. How do you prefer to give feedback?**
I prefer to give feedback verbally, since it is sometimes easier to talk in person and communicate ideas/tone over writing.

**4. How can someone motivate you to do something?**
I think talking about the outcome or impact of the work is helpful to motivate me, it definitely helps when I know how my work fits into the larger context of something.

Slack is good!
