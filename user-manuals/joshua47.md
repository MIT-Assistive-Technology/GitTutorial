# Joshua Rivera Camacho

In a few words, answer each question. Don’t think too hard :)

**1. What's your working style?**
I think it depends what I am working on, but I think hunkering down and toughing it out until I am done isn't an unusual trend -- although whether my productivity keeps up with that is a seperate matter. On projects with design or fab, it is very easy for me to fall into a time sink blackhole becuase I enjoy those two quite a bit.

**2. How do you prefer to receive feedback? (i.e. verbal vs. written, how often, anonymously?)**
I would probably say written, just because it is easier to refer back to later -- although I am most used to verbal feedback at this point. I don't have a specific timeframe for how often, but I think I appreciate a tiny bit of freedom. Anonymity isn't a huge deal to me (or at least i dont think).

**3. How do you prefer to give feedback?**
Maybe a bit hypocritical (lol) but I think I prefer giving verbal feedback because I find writing a bit tedious. I will admit -- however -- that writing is nice because it can sometimes offer a bit more reflection on the feedback.

**4. How can someone motivate you to do something?**
I have been trying to figure that out myself for the last three years at MIT ngl. Maybe prizes, maybe shame, maybe some other sort of emotional investment, maybe nothing at all. I am usually pretty motivated when I get a chance to build something though.

**5. What is your preferred method of communication?**
Probably through text, since I have a bad habit of overthinking responses -- particularly through slack or email.

**6. Foods you like? :D**
Currently obsessed with the marinated spicy beef bulgogi from hmart -- i like using if for bulgolgi bowls and grilled cheeses in particular. As a general trend I like spicy and spicy-sweet combos. I really like candy -- probably a bit too much -- so I stay away from them generally.
