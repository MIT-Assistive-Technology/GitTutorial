In a few words, answer each question. Don’t think too hard :)

1. What's your working style?

It depends on the task; if it concerns initial research/brainstorming of designs or ideas, then I prefer to work in a group setting. However, when it comes to actually designing or programming, I tend to prefer to work alone for a given part of the whole project.

2. How do you prefer to receive feedback? (i.e. verbal vs. written, how often, anonymously?)

I don't have a preference for the medium through which I receive the feedback. I would like regular feedback, but not too frequently.

3. How do you prefer to give feedback?

I prefer to write my feedback so that I can explain my thoughts better, and then through verbal communication as a supplement.

4. How can someone motivate you to do something?

I don't really know, but I would love to be able to work on something, whether it'd be a design or program, and say that I have made said thing.

5. What is your preferred method of communication?

Text (email is okay, but it tends to get buried easily).

6. Foods you like? :D

Ethiopian food! But other than that, I love pizza.
